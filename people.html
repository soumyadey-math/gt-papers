Everyone who appears as an author in the papers database, with last known affiliation, links to homepages, MathSciNet, zbMATH and the Mathematics Genealogy Project, publication counts and major prizes.

About this list

People are identified by their zbMATH Open author profile, so different spellings of the same name are merged. Papers here counts papers in this database; total publications is zbMATH's count of everything the person has published. Top journals are Annals of Mathematics, Acta Mathematica, Inventiones Mathematicae, Journal of the AMS and Publications Mathématiques de l'IHÉS.

Affiliations are the last known ones and may be out of date. Where an affiliation was taken from the author's own address on a recent arXiv paper, that paper is linked. MathSciNet links are shown where the author ID is known; MathSciNet needs a subscription. Prizes are major prizes only, so the list is not complete.

Corrections are very welcome. If anything about you or a colleague is wrong or missing, or you would like an entry changed or removed, please open an issue on GitHub or email the maintainer.
