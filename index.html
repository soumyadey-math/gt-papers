About this database

Records come from zbMATH Open. A paper is included when any of its MSC codes (primary or secondary) is one of the target codes shown as chips above, or when its title contains "mapping class" or "Teichmüller" (marked Found by title keyword). All years are covered for every journal.

  • Verified confirmed against the publisher's record in Crossref, or by two independent reads of the zbMATH record.
  • Checked read once; passed DOI, year and completeness checks.
  • Missing DOIs and a few errors in zbMATH (pages, year, issue) were corrected by hand against the publishers' pages.

The full data can be downloaded as BibTeX, CSV or JSON. Found an error or a missing paper? Please report it on GitHub.
